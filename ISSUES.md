Defects get a number (TNET-xxx), a severity, and stay listed until
fixed-with-test. (Master prompt §4.5)

Severity scale: `blocker` · `major` · `minor` · `cosmetic`.

When adding an issue, also note it in STATUS.md under "Built, unproven" or
"Missing" as appropriate, and never mark it resolved without a regression test
whose output is pasted in STATUS.md.
